# Help improve the publications

Useful contributions include a clear article correction, a broken-link report, an accessibility observation, or a small improvement to the reader website.

Start with the relevant public issue form. Give the public URL, edition, section or figure, explain what you observed, and describe the correction you suggest. A numerical correction should identify the relevant value and reasoning. Distinguish a new measurement from a recalculation of existing data, and a hypothesis from an observed result.

All issues and pull requests here are public. Do not attach private correspondence, research archives, review packets, personal information, credentials or files you do not have permission to share. Redact screenshots before sharing them. Security concerns use the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Keep pull requests focused. Explain the reader benefit, list the public files changed and describe what you checked. Keep the original evidence and meaning intact. A correction to a released article needs a visible edition/change record rather than a silent replacement. Do not include internal production manifests, source-archive fingerprints, unpublished implementation code or channel authoring kits.

Original publication content uses the notice in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt). Third-party material keeps its own terms. Explain the origin and permitted use of anything you propose to add. The publication license does not license research implementation code, and a proposed contribution is not permission to disclose someone else's private work. No separate DCO sign-off or contributor agreement is requested by this document.

Maintainers review contributions before merging. A passing automated check does not establish scientific validation, rights clearance or approval to publish new research.
