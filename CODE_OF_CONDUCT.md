# Working together

This is a place to discuss published research and improve the reader experience. Be respectful, specific and willing to correct a mistake.

Discuss claims, evidence and behavior rather than attacking a person. Explain unfamiliar terms, give useful references and distinguish what is known from what remains uncertain. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation and disclosure of another person's private information are not.

Keep comments relevant to the publication or website. Do not post private research, credentials or personal details. Use the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security concerns. If a conduct concern involves sensitive details, request a private contact route without publishing those details.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate public content, close disruptive discussions or limit participation using the platform's tools. These community expectations describe how this repository is moderated; they do not create a separate contributor agreement or promise a particular response time.
