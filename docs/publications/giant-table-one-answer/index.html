Computing by structure

Why Build a Giant Table When You Only Need One Answer?

Before buying more memory, check what the calculation actually needs to store.

Tin Ninkoviċ · 20 October 2026 · Cover edition 0.3.3-cover.1

Read the PDFDownload PDFCompanion files

What the article explains

A small worked example shows the difference between storing a table and calculating the answers it produces. The article then examines retained historical allocation measurements for one known structured calculation.

The two methods produced closely agreeing answers in that example. The reported memory values cover Python-tracked allocation, not the whole application, and do not establish a speed result.

Explore the supporting material

The companion contains six explanatory figures with text alternatives, selected historical observations, summary tables and plain methods. The article itself is supplied as one PDF.
